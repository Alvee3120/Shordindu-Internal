# Communication

- Wants replies written in English, not Chinese — corrects any Chinese-language response. Confidence: 0.85
- Wants status/progress reports stripped to the essentials: one line per item (e.g. per module) covering what was done and what remains, rather than long prose. Confidence: 0.55
- Prefers role/capability comparisons presented as a table (what each role can and cannot do) rather than prose. Confidence: 0.5
- When asking "what happens / what will the system do" in a specific scenario, wants the direct, concrete answer (actual current behavior) stated plainly and first — not an architecture review plus a fix proposal. Pushes back with "I didn't understand" when the reply jumps to recommendations. Confidence: 0.6
- Wants any unfamiliar third-party service, hostname, or workaround used during setup explained at the point it's introduced — being handed a live URL like an `nip.io` host with no explanation prompts "what is that?". Confidence: 0.45

- Prefers work interleaved with reporting: finish one chunk of work (e.g. one module), report it, then move to the next — rather than running several chunks and reporting them all at the end. Confidence: 0.55
